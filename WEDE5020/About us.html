Our Story:

The Village Bakery is more than just a bakery. It's a piece of community, made with heart, heritage, and a whole lot of flour.Our journey began in 1981 in Lenasia, south of Johannesburg where families and this business formed the heartbeat of the community. In times where freashly baked goods were not always easy to find, a small family dream grew into a trusted local bakery built on quality, affordablility and love.

What started as a single bakery has become a familiar name in the local area. The village bakery has become apart of everyday life for many people in Lenasia. Customers came not only for fresh bread, pies, cakes, and confectionery, but also for the warm service and familiar faces behind the counter.

Over the years, we have stayed true to our roots, and today the village bakery proudly serves customers from Lenasia, Fordsburg. As we continue to grow, our goal is to remain the trusted neighbourhood bakery that people can rely on for fresh, affordable, and quality baked goods. From our village to your table, thank you for being part of our story.

Missions statement:

Our mission is to provide the Fordsburg, Lenasia, and greater Johannesburg communities with freshly baked, affordable Halal bread, pies, cakes, and confectionery made daily with care, consistency, and pride. We are committed to serving our customers with quality products, friendly service, and honest value. Every loaf, pie, and sweet treat we make carries the same purpose: to bring people together through the simple joy of good food. As a local bakery, we believe in supporting the community that has supported us for decades. We aim to create opportunities for growth, build lasting relationships with our customers, and continue being a bakery that families can trust for generations to come.

Vision:

Our vision is to grow The Village Bakery into one of Johannesburg's most loved and trusted bakery brands, known for quality, affordability, Halal integrity, and strong community values. We see a future where The Village Bakery expands across Johannesburg, reaching more families, schools, businesses, and communities while keeping the same local heart that built our name. Our goal is to become a household name across the city, recognised not only for our baked goods, but also for the warmth, trust, and consistency behind every product. Looking ahead, we aim to grow beyond Johannesburg and become one of South Africa's leading bakery groups. We want to set the standard for local bakeries by blending tradition with innovation, staying true to our roots while improving the way we serve our customers. From Lenasia to every corner of South Africa, our vision is to keep baking with purpose, serving with pride, and growing with the people who made us who we are.
