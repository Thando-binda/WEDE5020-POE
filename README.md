The village bakery is small business in the south of Johannesburg, that is more than just a bakery. It is a piece of the community, made with heart, heritage and a whole lot of flour. With more than 40 years of history, The Bakery opened its doors to the community in 1981 and has continued to serve generations of customers with freshly baked, quality products. They aim to grow the business beyond just being a local business but a nationally recognised brand.
