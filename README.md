Proposal A: The Village Bakery
Organisation Overview
The Village Bakery is a real currently trading small business operating two retail bakeries. The original store at 56 Lilian Road, Fordsburg, and a second branch in Lenasia, south of Johannesburg. Trading as "The Village Bakery" since 1981, the business forms part of the Dafasa Group, a family enterprise developed by Farouk and Fawzia Akoojee, and is Halaal-certified (Bakerias.com, 2025).
No published mission or vision statement could be located in any public source. The statements below have therefore been drafted specifically for this proposal and are clearly marked as such; they would need to be confirmed or amended with the owners before being published on the live site.
•	Proposed mission: To provide the Fordsburg and Lenasia communities with freshly baked, affordable Halaal bread, pies and confectionery, made daily using recipes built up since 1981.
•	Proposed vision: To remain the trusted neighbourhood bakery of choice at both branches while growing wholesale supply to surrounding retailers.
Target audience: The local residents and families the wider Johannesburg South/West community; and small retailers and caterers interested in wholesale bread and pie supply.
Website Goals and Objectives
•	Establish a searchable, credible online presence beyond word-of-mouth.
•	Give customers a single, accurate source for both branch addresses, trading hours and directions.
•	Showcase the product range with photography and descriptions.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Goal	KPI
Online visibility	Reach unique visitors per month.
Search discoverability	Appear on the first page of Google results for bakeries in Jhb. 
Enquiries	Receive at least 10 wholesale enquiry-form submissions per month
Information accuracy	Reduce phone calls asking for basic directions or working hours, and a source of business reference.
Digital Presence Analysis
The Village Bakery has no functioning official website. Its only public digital presences are an auto-generated Google Business Site which offers only the default Google template with no custom branding, photography or navigation, and a Facebook page with a small number of reviews (Facebook, n.d.).
Strengths
•	An established nearly 50 years of trading history and loyal, repeat local customer base, evident from directory listings and reviews.
•	Independently verified Halaal certification, a strong trust signal for the target market.
Weaknesses and areas for improvement
•	No content that would help a customer outside the immediate suburb discover the business online a proper website address this directly.
•	No product photography or descriptions anywhere online the proposed Products page addresses this.
•	No way to submit a wholesale or catering enquiry online the proposed Enquiry page addresses this.
•	Inconsistent street-number information for the Fordsburg branch across different directories, a website under the bakery's own control would centralise one accurate, authoritative address.
Proposed Website Features and Functionality
Page	Purpose
Home (index.html)	Hero introduction with an image, brief overview, call to action, and navigation menu linking to all other pages.
About Us (about.html)	History since 1981, the Dafasa Group background, and Halaal certification.
Products (products.html)	Bread, pies, cakes, confectionery and wholesale range, with descriptions and photography.
Enquiry (enquiry.html)	Form general product enquiries
Contact (contact.html)	Verified contact details for both branches, an embedded map, and a contact note.
Design and User Experience
•	Colour scheme: warm bread-brown as the primary/header colour with a cream off-white background, evoking an artisan-bakery feel.
•	Typography: a friendly, rounded sans-serif for headings paired with a highly legible body font, sized for comfortable mobile reading.
•	Layout: single-column with large, easy-to-tap buttons for phoning the branch or opening the enquiry form, since most local customers are expected to browse on mobile data.
•	Navigation: one consistent five-item top menu appearing identically on every page.

Proposal B: Fournos Bakery Group
Organisation Overview
Fournos Bakery Group trading as Fournos, operated by Fournos Group (Pty) Ltd is a real, currently trading South African bakery, deli and coffee-shop chain that began as a small corner bakery in Rosebank, Johannesburg, in 1989 and has since grown to 14 stores across Gauteng (Fournos, 2026). The brand draws on Greek baking heritage alongside European and Mediterranean influences and has been voted Best Bakery in the Best of Joburg Readers' Choice Awards for 28 consecutive years (Fournos, 2026).
Fournos out a clear brand promise that functions as one: a stated commitment to freshness, quality, first-class ingredients and passionate service, built on treating customers "as family and friends" rather than transactions (Fournos, 2026). This is Fournos's own published positioning, not drafted for this proposal.
Target audience food and health-conscious consumers, families, and the corporate/catering community, including professionals using Fournos stores as informal meeting places (Fournos, 2026).
Website Goals and Objectives
•	Demonstrate a lightweight, fast-loading HTML/CSS/JS companion presence focused on core information (products, catering enquiries, branch contact) as a foundational-technology counterpart to the existing WordPress site.
•	Provide a simple, dedicated enquiry form for catering, platter and event bookings, distinct from the main site's shopping-cart checkout flow.
•	Present accurate, centralised contact information for at least two branches, addressing the assignment's Contact-page requirement directly.
•	Practise semantic HTML structure and accessible form design using a real, richly documented organisation as the case study.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Goal	KPI 
Catering enquiries	Receive at least 8 catering submissions per month
Page performance	Companion pages load in a few seconds on mobile, given the lighter vanilla HTML/CSS/JS build compared with the plugin-heavy production site
Accessibility	All images carry descriptive alt text and forms carry visible labels, verified with a manual accessibility check (Part 2/3 work)
Information accuracy	Zero discrepancies between the companion Contact page and the branch details on the live fournos.co.za site, checked monthly
Current Website Analysis
Fournos operates a full WordPress website with an integrated online ordering system, a Slider homepage carousel, a 14-branch "Find Us" locator with individually embedded Google Maps for each store, a downloadable PDF menu, and linked Facebook, Instagram and YouTube profiles (Fournos, 2026). 
Strengths
•	A mature, functioning online ordering system that is already driving a meaningful share of daily sales, a strong commercial proof point.
•	Efficient the site is detailed branch information such as address, phone, trading hours, embedded map for all 14 locations.
•	Strong social presence and an active presence across Facebook, Instagram and YouTube.
Weaknesses and areas for improvement
•	The single "Find Us" page loads 14 separate embedded Google Maps iframes on one page, which is likely to slow load times and increase mobile data usage - a companion page could offer a lighter, faster branch lookup.
•	There is no dedicated, simple enquiry form for catering, platter or event bookings distinct from the standard shopping-cart checkout - a gap the proposed Enquiry page addresses directly.
•	The "About Us" page is long-form and marketing-heavy rather than concise, making it harder for a mobile visitor to quickly find core facts.
•	Several decorative and icon images observed in the site's markup did not show clearly descriptive alt text in the fetched page content; this would need to be confirmed directly in a browser and is flagged here as a possible accessibility gap worth verifying, not a confirmed defect.
Proposed Website Features and Functionality
Page	Purpose
Home (index.html)	Hero introduction, brief overview, call to action, and navigation menu linking to all other pages.
About Us (about.html)	Concise, verified history (1989 Rosebank origin, 14 Gauteng branches, 28-year Best Bakery record) and brand promise.
Products (products.html)	Core categories drawn from the real Fournos range: artisan bread, pastries, deli, cakes and catering platters.
Enquiry (enquiry.html)	Dedicated catering/platter/event booking enquiry form (business enquiry type, per brief), separate from the main site's checkout.
Contact (contact.html)	Verified contact details for two representative branches (Rosebank and Parktown), an embedded map, and a contact form.
Design and User Experience
•	Colour scheme: warm bread-gold as the header colour with a cream off-white background, echoing the artisanal bakery identity visible on the existing site; exact brand hex values would be sampled directly from the Fournos logo during Part 2 CSS work.
•	Typography: a confident, slightly Mediterranean-influenced heading font paired with a clean, highly legible sans-serif body font.
•	Layout: mobile-first, single-column layout with a lightweight branch locator (two branches shown directly, rather than 14 stacked maps) to address the load-time weakness identified in Section 3.3.
•	Navigation: one consistent five-item top menu appearing identically on every page.
•	User experience and accessibility: strong colour contrast, descriptive alt text on every product photograph, clearly labelled form fields, and a concise About Us page for faster mobile scanning.
